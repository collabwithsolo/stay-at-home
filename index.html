A cozy hide-and-seek in the Ellington III

Stay at Home!

You’re Kai. Hide from Mom and Dad for three minutes. If they tag you, it’s chore time.

Seeing you isn’t enough — they have to actually touch you. You’re fast; they’re not. Baby Girl might crawl after you.

Difficulty: Easy: slower parents who check fewer spots.

How to Play

  1. Head start (3:00 → 2:50). Mom and Dad count to ten in the foyer. Find a spot!
  2. Hide. Step onto a glowing tile beside furniture and press E (or the Action button). You stay put until you press it again.
  3. Run or tiptoe. Kai is much faster than his parents, but running footsteps carry about 4 m. Tiptoe (C) shrinks that to 1.5 m and is still a little quicker than a searching parent.
  4. They have to touch you. If a parent sees you they shout and chase, but you’re only caught when they tag you — or reach into the exact spot you’re hiding in. Break their line of sight before you hide: if they watch you duck in, they’ll check it.
  5. Listen for hunches. Before checking a spot a parent says it out loud (“Maybe behind the sofa…”). No spot is checked twice — spots they’ve already checked (✓ on the pause map) are the safest places to move to.
  6. The household. Baby Girl crawls after you and giggles. Nori yips if you pass close. Rocky bats a noisy toy upstairs. Mom and Dad follow those sounds.
  7. Answer to get away. When a parent catches you, a question pops up — counting, colors, letters, shapes or a tiny sum. Tap the right picture and they count to six while you run. You get three ⭐ escapes per round on Easy, one on Normal.
  8. 🔥 Fire and ❄️ Freeze. Two power buttons sit right next to Hide (or press F / G). Use them any time you’re out in the open. Fire blows Mom and Dad back and makes them too hot to chase you; Freeze stops them still for longer. Each one recharges for a few seconds. If you get caught, answer the question right and pick a power to escape.
  9. Win by staying hidden until the clock hits 0:00.
